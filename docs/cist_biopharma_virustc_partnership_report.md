# PARTNERSHIP OPERATION REPORT: STRATEGIC ORGAN PURIFICATION PROTOCOL
**To:** Executive Leadership, CIST Biopharma  
**From:** Operations & Clinical Quality Assurance Teams  
**Date:** August 11, 2026  
**Subject:** Implementation of mandatory 5-hour VirusTC pre-delivery perfusion washing protocol  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
This report outlines the operational framework and strategic advantages of CIST Biopharma's formal partnership with VirusTC. To maximize graft survival and guarantee pathogen-free transport, CIST Biopharma has implemented a strict mandate: all harvested donor organs must undergo a minimum of 5 hours of continuous ex vivo machine perfusion and washing using VirusTC specialized fluid formulations before any delivery routes commence.

## 2. Partnership & Protocol Core Objectives
The collaboration combines CIST Biopharma's logistical network with VirusTC's advanced clearing technologies to achieve three primary goals:
* **Complete Bio-Burden Elimination:** Eradicate latent viral, bacterial, and endotoxin contaminants from donor tissues prior to transit.
* **Cellular Optimization:** Utilize the extended wash window to stabilize cellular osmotic pressure and mitigate ischemia-reperfusion injury (IRI).
* **Standardized Logistics:** Create a dependable baseline for transport readiness, ensuring every delivery route begins with a validated, fully optimized organ.

## 3. The 5-Hour Perfusion & Washing Framework
The 5-hour timeline is non-negotiable and structurally divided into clinical phases to safely transition the organ from harvest to the transport corridor.

| Phase | Timeline | Primary Objective | Clinical & Fluid Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Initial Flush & Equilibrate** | Hour 0.0 – 1.0 | Blood displacement and vascular cooling | Rapid infusion of chilled VirusTC baseline solution to flush residual donor blood and open capillary beds. |
| **Phase 2: Active Purge & Wash** | Hour 1.0 – 3.5 | Pathogen extraction and metabolic cleansing | Continuous mechanical perfusion. VirusTC active agents bind to and wash out cellular debris, micro-thrombi, and potential viral elements. |
| **Phase 3: Metabolic Stabilization** | Hour 3.5 – 5.0 | Substrate loading and final QC validation | Shift to nutrient-and-antioxidant-rich preservation fluids. Perfusion parameters (flow rate, resistance) are logged to clear the organ for transport. |

## 4. Operational Logistics & Route Integration
To ensure the 5-hour minimum hold does not compromise cold-ischemia limits, the following logistical rules are now active:
* **Perfusion-to-Route Handshake:** The logistical dispatch software is hard-locked against route activation until the mechanical perfusion system logs a minimum of 300 minutes of continuous operation.
* **Pre-Route Alert System:** At Hour 4.0 of the wash cycle, automated dispatch alerts are sent to couriers to synchronize arrival with the exact moment of machine disconnection.
* **On-Machine Transit Buffers:** If transit routes face unexpected regional delays, organs will remain actively perfusing on the VirusTC hardware rather than being transitioned prematurely to static storage.

## 5. Risk Mitigation & Compliance Standards
* **Interrupted Cycles:** If a perfusion pump fails or a fluid circuit pressure drop occurs before the 5-hour mark, the clock pauses. The organ cannot be cleared for delivery until the technical anomaly is resolved and the cumulative wash time equals or exceeds 5 hours.
* **Quality Assurance Documentation:** A chain-of-custody certificate detailing the full 5-hour perfusion metrics (pH levels, vascular resistance, temperature profiles) must be digitally signed by both the VirusTC technician and the CIST Biopharma logistics manager before loading onto delivery vehicles.

## 6. Conclusion
The integration of the VirusTC 5-hour purification protocol marks a critical evolutionary step for CIST Biopharma. By establishing this rigorous processing floor, the company elevates its clinical standards, guarantees unparalleled safety margins to recipient transplant centers, and secures its position as an industry leader in high-fidelity biopharmaceutical logistics.
