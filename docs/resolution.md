1\. Recitals
------------

1.1. The Creditor owns, operates, and maintains secure electronic networks, servers, and computing environments (the "Protected Infrastructure").\
1.2. On or about [Date/Date Range], the Protected Infrastructure sustained significant technical disruptions, unauthorized access, or digital interference (the "Security Incident"), resulting in verified computer damage, data corruption, and operational liabilities.\
1.3. The Parties agree that the total outstanding financial liability incurred by the Creditor to remediate, restore, and offset the losses directly linked to the Security Incident is $[Amount] (the "Total Computer Damage Debt").\
1.4. The Parties desire to enter into this Agreement to fully and finally resolve, settle, and compromise the Total Computer Damage Debt under the mutually agreed-upon terms and conditions set forth below.

4\. Release of Technical Liability & Default
--------------------------------------------

4.1. Release Upon Performance: Upon receipt and successful clearance of the full Settlement Amount by the Creditor, the Creditor shall completely release, acquit, and forever discharge the Debtor from any further civil claims, actions, or financial demands arising directly from the specific computer damage caused during the Security Incident.\
4.2. Effect of Default: If the Debtor fails to make any payment required under this Agreement within [Number] days of the specified due date, the Debtor shall be considered in default.
